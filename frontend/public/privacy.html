Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LockIn ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Distributed File System application located at https://www.lockindfs.site.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In, we collect the following basic profile information provided by Google OAuth:

  • Your email address (to identify your account and assign storage quota).
  • Your name (to personalize your dashboard experience).
  • Your profile picture URL (to display in your user dashboard).
  • A unique Google identifier (sub ID) to securely link your sessions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your secure user session.
  • To allocate and enforce storage quotas.
  • To display user profile information on your dashboard.

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your user profile information is stored in our coordinator database. Any files you upload are encrypted and distributed across our storage node cluster. We implement standard industry security practices to keep your session tokens and uploaded data secure.

4. Data Deletion

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data/files from our servers, please contact the administrator at lockinadmin@gmail.com, and your data will be permanently removed.
